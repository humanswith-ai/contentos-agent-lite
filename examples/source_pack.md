# Source pack — payment stack: payout speed vs fees (illustrative)

> Illustrative example. Sources are real public doc pages; figures are rounded for the example.

## Facts
- Stripe documents a default rolling payout schedule (commonly ~2 business days in mature markets) — https://stripe.com/docs/payouts (2025)
- Adyen supports configurable payout frequencies (daily / weekly / monthly) — https://docs.adyen.com/get-started-with-adyen/payment-glossary (2025)
- PayPal documents holds that can delay first payouts for new accounts — https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp (2024)
- Card processing fees cluster around ~2.9% + 30c for standard online cards — https://stripe.com/pricing (2025)

## Numbers
- ~2 business days default payout (Stripe)
- ~2.9% + 30c standard online card fee

## Audience questions
- How fast will money actually land in my account?
- Do new accounts get payout holds?
- Is a 0.1% fee difference worth a slower payout?
- Can I configure payout frequency?
- What breaks my cash flow at <20 employees?

## Competitor gaps
- Comparison posts rank processors by headline fee % and ignore payout timing and holds.

## Founder angle
- At <20 people, a 2-day vs 5-day payout swing hits runway harder than a 0.1% fee gap.
