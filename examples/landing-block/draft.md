## Why does payout speed matter more than a 0.1% fee gap?

For a SaaS team under 20 people, when money lands changes your runway more than the headline fee
does. Stripe's default payout schedule settles on a rolling ~2-business-day basis [1]; a processor
that pays out in 5 days, or holds new-account funds, quietly costs you working capital that a
0.1% fee difference never touches.

The standard online card fee clusters around 2.9% + 30c [2]. Most comparison posts rank processors
on that number alone — and skip the two things that actually break early cash flow:

- **Payout timing:** ~2 days (Stripe default) vs 5+ days elsewhere.
- **Holds:** new accounts can have first payouts delayed.

## What should a founder check first?
Before the fee table, ask the processor two questions: how fast do funds settle by default, and
do new accounts get held?

**Is a slower payout ever worth a lower fee?** Rarely under 20 people — runway is the constraint.
