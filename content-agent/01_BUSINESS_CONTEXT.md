# 01 · Business Context — fill this once

The agent cannot write in your voice or make safe claims without this. Fill every field below
(replace the `>` prompts). This is the foundation; keep it and reuse it for every piece.

## ICP (who you write for)
> One specific buyer. Role, company stage/size, the job they're trying to do, what they already tried.
> Example: "Series A SaaS founders (10-50 people) choosing a payment stack, burned by hidden fees."

## Offer (what you sell)
> The product/service, the core outcome, the price band, the one thing that makes you different.

## Proof / evidence (what you can stand behind)
> Concrete results, numbers, named clients (if allowed), case outcomes, your own experience.
> The agent may ONLY assert things backed here or in the source pack.

## Brand voice
> 3-5 adjectives + 1-2 "we sound like / we never sound like" examples. Paste a paragraph you'd
> be happy to publish, so the agent can match rhythm and register.

## Claim-approval policy
> What may be asserted freely vs what needs a source/disclaimer. Forbidden claims (legal, medical,
> financial, competitor put-downs). The agent treats anything outside this as a P0.

## Founder angle
> Your unique point of view / contrarian take / lived experience the audience can't get elsewhere.
> This is what makes the text yours and not generic.
